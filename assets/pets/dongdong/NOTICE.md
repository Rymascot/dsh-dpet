# Dongdong pet (assets/pets/dongdong/)

`dongdong.glb` depicts Dongdong (东东), the mascot of Dalian Neusoft University of Information (大连东软信息学院, DNUI). The mascot's name, image, and likeness are owned by DNUI; its students may use the mascot freely, which is the basis on which this pet is contributed by ry, a DNUI student.

The asset is a non-commercial student work and is NOT covered by the repository's code license: do not use it commercially, and obtain permission from the university for any use outside that scope.

The 3D model was generated from the mascot artwork with Tencent Hunyuan 3D and then decimated and re-encoded (mesh simplification, WebP textures) with glTF-Transform for real-time use.
