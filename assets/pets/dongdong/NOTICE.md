因此这里的素材**不适用**本仓库的代码许可证（Apache-2.0）：不得用于商业用途，也不得单独提取、再分发或放进其他项目。如需在本项目之外使用，请联系大连东软信息学院取得许可。

3D 模型由腾讯混元 3D 根据吉祥物形象生成，再经 glTF-Transform 减面并将贴图转码为 WebP，以便实时渲染。

# Dong Xiaolong pet (assets/pets/dongdong/)

`dongdong.glb` depicts Dong Xiaolong (东小龙), the mascot of Dalian Neusoft University of Information (大连东软信息学院, DNUI). The mascot's name, image, and likeness are owned by DNUI. The university lets its students use the mascot, and ry, the author of this project, is a DNUI student; this project only has a right to use Dong Xiaolong. It does not own the mascot and cannot grant any rights in it to others.

These assets are therefore NOT covered by the repository's code license (Apache-2.0): do not use them commercially, and do not extract, redistribute, or reuse them in other projects. For any use outside this project, ask Dalian Neusoft University of Information for permission.

The 3D model was generated from the mascot artwork with Tencent Hunyuan 3D and then decimated and re-encoded (mesh simplification, WebP textures) with glTF-Transform for real-time use.
